SHORT BIO
Sahana Aster Kōwhai is a fictional palaeoclimatologist whose research reconstructs Southern Ocean change from
marine sediment archives.

RESEARCH FOCUS
Southern Ocean palaeoceanography across glacial terminations, with an emphasis on multiproxy reconstructions,
reproducible chronology and equitable Antarctic field partnerships.
